# Forward Movement

Forward Movement is a Birmingham-based registered charity (No. 1191828) supporting young people and people in need through education, training, performing arts, media, sport, community programmes, employability, housing support and opportunities for personal development.

## Website

https://www.forwardmovement.org.uk/

## About the charity

Forward Movement operates in Birmingham and works to create opportunities that help people build skills, confidence, independence and participation in society.

The charity's public registration information is available from the Charity Commission for England and Wales:

https://register-of-charities.charitycommission.gov.uk/en/charity-search/-/charity-details/5164644/full-print
